03 · Contrast

Measured contrast

WCAG 2.2 ratios computed in the browser: OKLCH → OKLab → linear sRGB → relative luminance. Text needs 4.5:1, focus rings and control outlines 3:1. Hairlines are decorative and never carry meaning alone.

04 · Status & domain

Status, AI and variables

Each status has three roles: a mark (--status-x) for dots and icons, text (-foreground), and a tint (-bg). Marks share one lightness — the Y column shows their relative luminance stays in a narrow band.

05 · Surfaces

Surfaces and elevation

Inside the page, depth comes from hairlines and a one-step tone shift — no shadows. Shadows and glass belong only to layers that float: popovers, menus, the command palette, toasts and dialogs. In dark, higher means lighter.

08 · Motion

Motion

Refined and alive, never stiff. Everything eases on a spring-like curve, travels 2–6px, scales from 0.98, and settles in 120–240ms. Exits are 30% faster than entrances. With reduced motion, the same moments become short opacity fades. Use the switch in the top bar to compare.

Durations
Distance · scale · stagger
Rules of thumb
Enter: fade + 4–6px rise + 0.98 → 1, spring. Exit: × 0.7, accelerate, 2px. Hover: tint or 1px lift. Never layout shift. Press: scale 0.98 on a snappy spring. Lists: 24ms stagger, first 8 only. Never block input. Never linear, except loops.

Easing and springs

Same distance, each curve at its own token duration. Springs are linear() approximations of real damped springs.

Enter and exit

Fade, 6px rise, 0.98 → 1 on spring-smooth. Exit is faster and smaller.

09 · Focus

Focus ring

Press Tab. A crisp 2px outline in --ring with a 2px offset — never a glow, never removed. Rows and cells use an inset offset so the ring is never clipped. Invalid fields keep the ring but switch it to destructive.
